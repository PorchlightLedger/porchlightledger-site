Questions

Will it work on my computer?

Works in Microsoft Excel 2010 or newer (Windows and Mac) and Google Sheets (free). Apple Numbers and LibreOffice not fully tested.

How do I open it in Google Sheets?

Upload the .xlsx to Google Drive, open it, then choose File > Save as Google Sheets. In Excel, just open the .xlsx file.

Where do I start?

Start on the “Start Here” tab and fill in the pale-yellow cells. Everything else calculates for you. Each planner also comes with an example file filled with sample data so you can see how it works.

Can I print it?

Yes. Every planner includes printable PDF pages in US Letter and A4. Print them at home or at a print shop.

How do I get my files?

Download them from your Etsy Purchases page right after checkout. It's a digital download only, so nothing is shipped.

Is this financial advice?

No. These are planning and tracking tools only, not financial advice. The planner adds up the numbers you type; it doesn't tell you what to spend, save or pay.
